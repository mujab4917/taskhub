You can also open any task and choose a reminder: at the due time, 10 or 30 minutes before, 1 hour before or 1 day before.

What you see when a reminder fires

  • A pop-up inside TaskHub with three buttons: Done, Snooze 10 min and Dismiss.
  • A desktop notification, if you allow notifications in your browser.
  • A short sound, which you can turn off in Settings.

Install it so it runs like an app

  1. Open TaskHub in Chrome or Edge

    Click the install icon in the address bar, or the Install button in the sidebar.

  2. Allow notifications

    TaskHub asks the first time you add a task with a reminder. You can test it from Settings.

  3. Start it with your computer

    In your system settings, let the installed app launch at startup and keep it minimized.

Reminders when the app is closed

TaskHub shows reminders while it is open, and a minimized window is fine. A web app cannot wake itself when it is fully closed. For that case, use Export to calendar in Settings. It creates a calendar file with an alarm for each task, and your calendar app, such as Outlook, Google Calendar or Apple Calendar, then handles the alerts.

Common questions

Can I set more than one reminder on a task?

Each task has one reminder time. For extra nudges, snooze the reminder when it fires, or add a checklist step with its own task.

What happens if I miss a reminder because the app was closed?

When you open TaskHub again, reminders from the last six hours that did not fire are shown. Older ones are skipped so you are not buried in stale alerts.

Does TaskHub send email reminders?

Not yet. Reminders appear in the app and as desktop notifications. For alerts outside the app, export your tasks to a calendar file and let your calendar app handle the alarms.

Can I change the default reminder?

Yes. In Settings, choose the default reminder for new tasks: none, at the due time, 10 or 30 minutes before, or 1 hour before.
