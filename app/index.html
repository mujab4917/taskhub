How TaskHub works

Type naturally in the top box and press Enter.

Call client tomorrow 3pmDue tomorrow at 15:00
Send report in 2h !highDue in 2 hours, high priority
Review titles fri 11am #bayut @saraAssigned to Sara, category “bayut”
Standup notes +teamVisible to the whole team
Weekly report mon 9am every weekRepeats every week

Privacy. Everything you create is private. Only you, and the person you assign it to, can see it. Use “Who can see this” on a task to share it with specific people or the whole team. Goals work the same way.
