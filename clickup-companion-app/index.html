TaskHub/ClickUp companion

A personal task manager that works alongside ClickUp

Your team tracks the official work in ClickUp. TaskHub holds your own plan around it: priorities for today, reminders and notes you do not want on a shared board.

Try it free

Why keep a personal layer

A shared tracker answers what the team is doing. It rarely answers what you should do first this morning. TaskHub is where you decide that, then link back to the ticket for the details.

Link tickets to tasks

Paste a ticket URL into the quick-add box, or into a task's link field. The task shows a ClickUp chip that opens the ticket in a new tab.

Copy a title and description for ClickUp

Draft the ticket in TaskHub, open More, and choose Copy title and Copy description. The description includes notes, priority, due date, category, assignee, link and a checklist, formatted so it pastes cleanly.

Import your tasks from a CSV export

Export tasks from ClickUp as a CSV file and choose Import from ClickUp in TaskHub. You can limit the import to tasks assigned to you and skip completed ones. Each imported task keeps its name, notes, due date, priority, status and a link back.

What TaskHub does not do

  • It does not sync both ways. Changes in one place do not update the other.
  • It is an independent product. TaskHub is not affiliated with or endorsed by ClickUp, and ClickUp is a trademark of its owner.

Common questions

Does TaskHub sync with ClickUp?

No. There is no two-way sync. You link tickets, copy text across and import from a CSV export.

Which columns does the CSV import read?

Task name, description, due date, priority, status, assignees, tags or list name, and the task ID or URL. Column names vary between exports, so check the preview before you import.

Do I need a ClickUp account to use TaskHub?

No. TaskHub works on its own. The ClickUp features are conveniences for people who also use it.

Are imported tasks visible to my team?

No. Imported tasks are private to you until you assign or share them.
