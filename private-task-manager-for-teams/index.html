A private task manager for teams

Most team tools assume everything should be visible. TaskHub assumes the opposite: your list is yours until you decide otherwise.

Try it free

What private means in TaskHub

Every task and goal you create starts private. You choose who else can see it, one task at a time.

What teammates can and cannot do

  • An assignee can update status, tick checklist steps and comment. They cannot change the title, date, visibility or who else can see it.
  • Only the owner can delete a task or change who sees it.
  • Comments and activity appear only on tasks a person can already see.

Reports and dashboards respect privacy

The team dashboard and standup page count only tasks that are assigned or shared. Your private tasks never appear there. When you copy your own standup, you can include or leave out your private tasks. The team standup never includes them.

An honest note about administrators

Privacy rules are enforced in the database, not only in the screen, so teammates and workspace owners cannot read your private tasks through the app. As with any hosted application, the person who administers the underlying database can technically access stored data. If that matters to your team, decide who holds that role.

Who this suits

Teams where people keep a personal plan alongside shared work: marketing, SEO and content teams, agencies and any group that does not want every note visible to everyone. See how SEO teams use it.

Common questions

Can I make a shared task private again?

Yes. The owner can change who sees a task at any time. Once it is private again, teammates lose access, except an assignee, who keeps access while the task is assigned to them.

Do goals have the same privacy options?

Goals are either private to you or shared with the whole team. When a goal is shared, teammates can see its title, target date, notes and milestones.

What does an assignee see if I give them a private task?

They see that one task. They can update its status, tick checklist steps and comment. They do not see your other private tasks.

Can I check who has access to a task?

Yes. Open the task and look at the setting called Who can see this. It shows whether the task is private, shared with named people or visible to the whole team.
