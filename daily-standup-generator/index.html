The three-part standup format

Most teams use the same shape: what you finished, what you will do next, and what is stopping you. Here is a ready-to-paste example.

Standup: Ali Khan, Tue, Oct 13
Yesterday:
- Rewrote category page titles [Bayut]
- Sent sitemap fixes to dev [Zameen]
Today:
- Review hreflang tags [OLX]
- Draft content brief [Content]
Blockers:
- Canonical fix (waiting on dev)

How TaskHub builds it

  • Finished since yesterday comes from tasks you completed.
  • Working on today comes from tasks due today or marked In progress.
  • Waiting or overdue comes from tasks marked Waiting and anything past its due date.

Press Copy my standup and paste it into your team chat.

A weekly report from the same data

The weekly report groups finished work by category, lists what is in progress, previews next week and adds tracked time if you use the timer.

Tips for updates people read

  • Write what changed, not a diary of everything you touched.
  • Name the blocker, who can remove it and by when.
  • Keep it short. Fifty to a hundred words is enough for most days.

Private tasks stay private

Your own standup can include your private tasks or leave them out with one checkbox. The team standup, which combines everyone, never includes a private task. Read more about how privacy works.

Common questions

Which tasks appear under Working on today?

Tasks due today and tasks marked In progress. A task with no date that is not In progress does not appear.

What counts as yesterday?

Anything you completed since midnight at the start of yesterday.

Can I edit the standup before sending it?

The copy goes to your clipboard as plain text, so you can paste it into chat and edit it there.

Can a manager see everyone's standup?

The standup page shows teammates only through tasks they have assigned or shared. Private tasks never appear.
